Most medspas carry whatever the distributor pushes that quarter. We don't operate that way. Before any product reaches our retail wall, at least two clinicians on our team have used it personally and recommended it to patients — and seen results.

All six lines we carry are backed by peer-reviewed clinical data. We've met the researchers. We've heard the trial results. That's not common in retail aesthetics — it's the floor for us.

Common questions

Product FAQs

Can I purchase products without being a client?

Yes — with one condition. For prescription-adjacent products (notably the Obagi Nu-Derm System), we require a brief consultation before purchase. Not because of policy, but because misusing those products can cause real skin damage. The consultation is complimentary and takes about 15 minutes. For everything else, you're welcome to come in and shop without a prior appointment.

Do you offer samples?

For most products, yes — we keep small samples of several serums and moisturizers available for clients who want to patch test before committing to a full-size purchase. Availability varies. We don't sample every product in our line, particularly those with strong actives, because a true efficacy assessment requires at least 4–6 weeks of consistent use — not a 3-day trial.

How do I know which products are right for my skin?

We offer complimentary product consultations with any in-office service — and standalone product consultations for $45 (applied as a retail credit if you purchase that day). Our aestheticians will assess your skin, ask about your current routine, and give you a short, honest list of what would actually help. We don't do upselling. If your current routine is working, we'll tell you that too.

Do you ship products?

Not currently. All retail purchases happen in-office at our Stafford, VA location. This is partly practical — shipping medical-grade skincare, particularly vitamin C serums, requires specific temperature and light controls that we can't guarantee in transit. It's also partly intentional: we prefer that retail purchases happen in conversation, not in a shopping cart. We're working on a limited local delivery option — ask us when you're in.
