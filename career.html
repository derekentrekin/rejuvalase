About the role

We're looking for a licensed registered nurse who brings clinical precision, genuine patient empathy, and the kind of curiosity that makes a great aesthetic injector — whether you're brand new to aesthetics or transitioning from another clinical specialty. This is a growth-track position: we invest in training, mentorship, and Allergan certification for the right candidate.

At Rejuvalase, our injectors don't work from a script. They work from a deep understanding of facial anatomy, patient goals, and the judgment to know when less is more. Our philosophy is conservative by design — we believe the most impressive results are the ones that don't announce themselves. You'll be expected to hold that standard, push back when a patient wants more than they need, and take the time to educate rather than just execute.

Part-time hours with genuine flexibility. We're a small team, which means your voice matters from day one. If you're interested in growing into a full-time or lead role over time, there's a clear path — and we'll build it with you, not for you.

Responsibilities

  • Perform neuromodulator (Botox, Dysport) and hyaluronic acid filler treatments under medical director supervision
  • Conduct thorough patient consultations, including intake assessment, treatment history review, and goal-setting conversations
  • Assess for retained filler using clinical palpation and visual analysis; recommend hyaluronidase dissolution when appropriate
  • Maintain accurate, HIPAA-compliant patient records in our EMR system (Jane App)
  • Provide pre- and post-treatment education to patients, including aftercare instructions and realistic outcome expectations
  • Participate in clinical team meetings and contribute to protocol development
  • Maintain a clean, safe, and organized treatment environment per OSHA and clinical standards
  • Respond to patient questions and concerns in a timely, compassionate, and clinically sound manner

Requirements

  • Active Registered Nurse (RN) license in the Commonwealth of Virginia — no exceptions
  • Minimum 3 years of clinical nursing experience (acute care, surgical, or aesthetic)
  • Strong foundation in facial anatomy; aesthetic experience preferred but not required for the right candidate
  • Demonstrated ability to have difficult conversations — including telling a patient no
  • Excellent documentation habits and attention to detail in clinical record-keeping
  • Commitment to ongoing education and willingness to complete Allergan certification training
  • Warm, professional, and patient-centered communication style — no hard-sell instincts
  • Availability for a minimum of 2–3 shifts per week, including at least one weekend shift
