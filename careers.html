Competitive pay + tips

Our providers are compensated above market rate, with a transparent tip structure that meaningfully increases take-home. We believe the best practitioners deserve to be paid like it.

All continuing education credits, certification renewals, and approved training programs are funded by Rejuvalase. We send our providers to Allergan training, national conferences, and advanced technique workshops.

We don't track product placed, services sold, or upsell rates. Our providers do what's right for the patient, period. This is non-negotiable and it won't change.

Flexible scheduling

We work with your life, not the other way around. Part-time, full-time, and modified schedules are available for most roles. We're a small team — we figure things out together.

Our culture

Small team.
High standards.
Real support.

We're not a franchise. We're not a volume-driven production floor. We're a small, intentional practice that believes the quality of care we give our patients is a direct reflection of how well we take care of our team.

At Rejuvalase, providers are trusted professionals, not managed metrics. Your clinical judgment matters. Your schedule matters. Your growth matters. We make decisions together, support each other openly, and hold ourselves to standards that don't bend to convenience.

If you're looking for a place where you can do your best work, be seen for doing it, and build something you're proud of — we'd like to meet you.
