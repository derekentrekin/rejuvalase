What it is

Safe, proven, and remarkably precise.

Botox Cosmetic (onabotulinumtoxinA) is an FDA-approved neuromodulator that temporarily relaxes specific facial muscles responsible for dynamic wrinkles — those lines formed by years of smiling, squinting, and frowning. By interrupting the nerve signal to targeted muscles, Botox smooths forehead lines, crow's feet, and frown lines (the "elevens") without affecting the natural expressions that make you, you.

At Rejuvalase, every Botox treatment begins with a thorough facial assessment. Our physician evaluates your muscle movement, skin quality, and aesthetic goals before placing a single unit. We dose conservatively and check your results at a complimentary two-week follow-up — because we believe the best outcome is one that looks like nothing happened at all.

Results typically appear within 3–7 days and last 3–4 months, with many clients finding that regular treatments extend duration over time as muscles gently adapt.

Is Botox right for you?

Botox is one of the most widely studied cosmetic treatments available. It is appropriate for a broad range of adults, though a consultation ensures it aligns with your specific health profile and goals.

  • Adults 21+ with dynamic (movement-related) wrinkles
  • Those seeking a refreshed look without surgery or filler
  • Preventive clients in their late 20s–30s managing early line formation
  • Anyone frustrated by forehead lines, crow's feet, or "elevens"
  • Clients looking to soften a gummy smile or reduce jaw tension (masseter)
  • People who want results that look natural, not frozen

Botox FAQ

Yes. Botox Cosmetic has been FDA-approved for cosmetic use since 2002 and has one of the most extensive safety records of any aesthetic treatment. When administered by trained medical professionals, adverse effects are rare and typically mild — minor bruising, temporary headache, or slight swelling at the injection site. At Rejuvalase, all injections are physician-performed or directly supervised, minimizing risk further.

Most clients describe the sensation as a brief pinch — similar to a small insect sting that resolves in under a second. We use ultra-fine needles and inject slowly to minimize discomfort. A topical numbing cream is available on request, though most clients find it unnecessary for this procedure. The entire treatment typically takes under 20 minutes.

Results typically appear within 3–7 days of treatment and last 3–4 months on average. Duration varies based on individual metabolism, muscle strength, and the areas treated. Many clients find that with consistent treatment over time, results extend slightly as muscles gradually adapt. We recommend scheduling your next appointment before full movement returns to maintain optimal results.

Avoid blood thinners (aspirin, ibuprofen, fish oil, vitamin E) for 5–7 days before treatment if possible, as these increase bruising risk. Arrive with a clean face — no makeup on the treatment area. Avoid alcohol for 24 hours beforehand. If you have an important event, we recommend scheduling Botox at least 2 weeks prior to allow full results to settle. Let us know about any medications or supplements at your consultation.

There is essentially no downtime. You can return to work and most normal activities immediately. We ask clients to avoid strenuous exercise, lying flat, and touching or massaging the treated area for 4 hours post-treatment. Skip saunas, hot yoga, or intensive cardio for 24 hours. Avoid facial massages for one week. Small pinpoint marks or mild redness at injection sites typically resolve within 30–60 minutes.
