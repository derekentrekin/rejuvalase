Before you visit

Booking questions.

Everything you need to know before your first appointment. More questions? We're happy to answer by phone or email.

Is the consultation really free?

Yes — always and without exception. We do not charge for consultations and will never pressure you into scheduling or purchasing during one. Our goal is to give you accurate, personalized information so you can make the right decision for yourself, on your own timeline.

What is your cancellation policy?

We ask for 24 hours notice for cancellations or rescheduling of treatment appointments. Consultations can be cancelled or moved at any time with no fee. Life happens — if you need to cancel last-minute, just let us know as soon as you can and we'll work something out.

How far in advance should I book?

Consultations can often be scheduled within 3–5 business days. Treatment appointments with specific providers — especially Nadia for injectables — may book out 2–3 weeks, particularly on Fridays and Saturdays. If you have a specific date in mind (event, wedding, etc.), we recommend reaching out at least 6–8 weeks in advance.

Do you accept insurance?

Aesthetic treatments are elective and not covered by medical insurance. We accept all major credit cards, FSA/HSA cards for eligible treatments, and offer third-party financing through CareCredit and Cherry. We'll outline all payment options clearly before any treatment begins.
