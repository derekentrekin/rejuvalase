Medical Director

Dr. Maya Chen, MD.

Dr. Chen completed her medical training at Johns Hopkins and spent seven years in academic aesthetic practice before founding Rejuvalase in 2019. Her clinical focus is facial anatomy and the structural changes underlying visible aging — an approach that favors precision over volume and subtraction over addition.

She sits on the Virginia Society of Plastic Surgeons' aesthetic advisory panel, publishes clinical case studies on injectable outcomes, and conducts quarterly training reviews with every injector on staff. Patients consistently note that her consultations feel less like a sales appointment and more like a conversation with a trusted physician — which is exactly what they are.

FAQ

Your questions, answered.

Still have a question that isn't here? Call us at 540·555·0134 or send a note to hello@rejuvalase.com — a member of our clinical team responds within one business day.

How do I know which provider is right for my treatment?

During your initial consultation, Dr. Chen or a senior clinician will review your goals and recommend the most appropriate provider based on the treatment indicated. Every plan is physician-supervised regardless of who performs it.

Do all of your injectors have physician oversight?

Yes. Virginia law and our internal protocols require that all injectable procedures performed by RNs operate under a supervising physician's standing orders. Dr. Chen reviews all treatment plans and is available for consultation on every appointment day.

How often does the team receive additional training?

We hold quarterly internal training reviews led by Dr. Chen. Additionally, all clinical staff attend at least one accredited aesthetic medicine conference per year and complete manufacturer-led certification updates whenever new devices or formulations are introduced.

Can I request a specific provider for my appointment?

Absolutely. Once you've established care with a specific provider, we'll always schedule you with them when available. We'll also let you know in advance if scheduling requires an adjustment, so you can decide how to proceed.

What's the difference between a nurse injector and a physician injector?

Our RN injectors are specialty-trained aesthetic nurses with thousands of procedures under their belts — Nadia alone has over 6,000 injectable treatments. The key distinction at Rejuvalase is that both operate under the same clinical protocols and physician oversight, ensuring consistent safety and outcomes regardless of provider.
