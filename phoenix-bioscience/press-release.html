Phoenix Bioscience opens pre-orders for Corvex™, a daily mobility chew for working dogs.

For immediate release · May 2026

AUSTIN, Texas — Phoenix Bioscience, a science-backed animal performance health company, today opened pre-orders for Corvex™, a once-daily soft chew designed to support mobility, joint comfort, and recovery in working, sporting, and active dogs.

Corvex™ combines nine active ingredients on a spray-dried porcine plasma platform, including the research-backed branded actives Levagen+®, GlycOmega™, NMX1000®, and UC-II®. Each 60-count jar contains 7g soft chews formulated under veterinary leadership, with dosing rationale and ingredient reasoning published openly in the company's research library.

"The dogs that work hardest were being served by products built for the ones that don't," said the Phoenix Bioscience founding team. "Corvex™ is our answer: a formula we can defend ingredient by ingredient, sold directly to the people who work their dogs, with claims that stay inside the evidence."

Phoenix Bioscience will sell exclusively direct-to-consumer in its first year — no retail and no third-party marketplaces — a decision the company describes as central to formula integrity and batch traceability. Corvex™ is available by subscription or one-time purchase, with pre-orders shipping in launch order. The company's second product, HydraDrive™, a single-serve hydration and recovery gel for dogs, is expected to follow.

About Phoenix Bioscience

Phoenix Bioscience builds science-backed performance health products for working, sporting, and active dogs. Formulas are developed under veterinary leadership, tested in real field conditions, and sold directly to consumers. The company's mission is simple: helping dogs live their best lives.
