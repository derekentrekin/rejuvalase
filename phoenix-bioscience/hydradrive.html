Why hydration

Water alone isn't a plan.

Hard-working dogs lose more than water — and many won't drink enough of it when it matters most. HydraDrive™ is designed as a palatable, lappable gel that helps support fluid intake, electrolyte balance, and recovery through heat, exertion, and travel.
