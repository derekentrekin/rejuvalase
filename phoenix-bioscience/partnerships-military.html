Partnerships · Military & working K9

For the dogs that serve.

Working K9s operate in conditions most supplements were never designed for — heat, travel, and sustained work without an off-season. We build for those conditions, and we support the handlers and units who live in them.

Who it serves

Units and handlers.

Military working dog programs, law enforcement K9 units, search-and-rescue teams, and individual handlers — active or retired. If your dog's work is a job description, you qualify.

Built for field conditions

Formats that travel.

Soft chews that hold up in a kit bag. Single-serve hydration sachets that don't need a bowl, a scoop, or clean counter space. Shelf-stable, batch-traceable, and packable — because the field is the design constraint, not an afterthought.

The program

Practical support.

Program pricing for units and departments, simplified procurement paperwork, and handler pricing for personal dogs. Quietly, and on your terms — we don't use anyone's service as marketing material.
