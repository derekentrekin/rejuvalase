Direct to consumer

No retail. No Amazon. On purpose.

Phoenix sells one way in Year 1: directly to you. That's not a limitation we're working around — it's the model the product requires.

A direct relationship

When you buy from Phoenix, you're talking to the people who formulated the product — for questions, feedback, and support. No distributor between us, no reseller guessing at answers.

Formula integrity

Retail margins bend formulas — cheaper actives, smaller inclusions, bigger claims. Selling direct means the formula answers to the evidence and the dogs, not to a buyer's spreadsheet.

Batch traceability

Every jar ships from our own fulfillment, tied to its batch. No gray-market listings, no expired stock in a third-party warehouse, no counterfeit risk. You get what we made — provably.

The subscription

Consistency is the active ingredient.

Joint-support formulas work through daily, uninterrupted use — which makes the empty jar the biggest failure mode in the category. The Corvex™ subscription exists to remove it.

It's cheaper too, but that's the second reason, not the first.

  • Subscriber pricing on every jar — $54 vs. $64 one-time
  • A fresh jar every 60 days — timed to the last chew, not before
  • Pause, skip, or cancel anytime — from your account, in seconds
  • First access to new formulas, including HydraDrive™
What to expect

Shipping, fulfillment, support.

Shipping

Orders ship from our fulfillment center within two business days, with tracking on every package. Subscription renewals ship automatically on your schedule.

Fulfillment

Product is stored and packed in climate-controlled conditions, batch-tracked from production to your door. If a jar arrives damaged, we replace it — no forms, no debate.

Support

Real people, one business day or less. Questions about the formula get routed to the people who built it, not to a script.
