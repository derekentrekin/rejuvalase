Why plasma

The platform is plasma.

Most joint chews start with glucosamine and stop thinking. Corvex™ starts with spray-dried porcine plasma — a source of functional proteins, including immunoglobulins, studied in animal nutrition for its role in supporting gut integrity, normal immune balance, and recovery from exertion.

The eight actives that follow are built on that base — each one there for a reason we can explain.

The formula

Nine actives. Zero filler logic.

Every ingredient below is in the jar for a mechanism, not a marketing line. Per-chew amounts will be published with the final approved label.

Spray-dried porcine plasmaThe platform

Functional proteins, including immunoglobulins, studied in animal nutrition for supporting gut integrity, normal immune balance, and recovery from hard work. The foundation the rest of the formula stands on.

Levagen+®Palmitoylethanolamide (PEA)

A bioavailability-enhanced form of PEA, a fatty-acid compound produced naturally in the body and studied for its role in supporting a normal inflammatory response and everyday joint comfort.

GlycOmega™Green-lipped mussel

New Zealand green-lipped mussel, a whole-food source of omega-3 fatty acids and glycosaminoglycans associated with joint comfort and mobility support in dogs.

NMX1000®Standardized branded active

A standardized, research-supported active included in the formula's recovery lane — chosen, like everything here, for documented rationale rather than label appeal. Full sourcing details publish with the label.

UC-II®Undenatured type II collagen

A low-dose, undenatured collagen studied for supporting joint comfort and flexibility through a mechanism distinct from — and complementary to — traditional glucosamine and chondroitin.

Glucosamine HClJoint structure

A building block of cartilage and connective tissue, included to help maintain normal joint structure over the long seasons that add up.

Chondroitin sulfateCartilage resilience

A glycosaminoglycan that pairs with glucosamine to help support cartilage resilience and the cushioning joints rely on under load.

MSMMethylsulfonylmethane

A dietary sulfur donor commonly used to support comfort after exertion — the kind of exertion working dogs consider a normal Tuesday.

Hyaluronic acidJoint lubrication

A key component of synovial fluid, included to help support the lubrication and smooth movement of hard-working joints.

Questions

Straight answers.

The 7g format gives us room to carry meaningful inclusion levels of nine actives in a single daily chew, instead of splitting the dose across a handful of smaller pieces. One chew, once a day, is also the routine most likely to actually happen — and consistency is where supplements do their work.

Give one chew daily, with or without food, at whatever time fits your routine. Most dogs take it as a treat. For dogs under veterinary care or on medication, check with your veterinarian before adding any supplement.

Honestly: give it time. Joint-support ingredients are studied over weeks, not days — many owners look for changes across four to eight weeks of consistent daily use. Corvex™ supports normal mobility and comfort; it is not a medication, and it won't act like one.
