The process

What to expect

Step 01

Pre-treatment consultation

Before your appointment, we review your skin history, medications, and goals. BBL has some contraindications — we want to know about them before you're in the chair, not after. This is included and takes about 15 minutes.

Step 02

Treatment day

BBL first, then HydraFacial. The sequence matters — the HydraFacial calms and protects right after the light energy. Your total chair time is approximately 90 minutes. Plan for mild redness that resolves within a few hours.

Step 03

Follow-up at six weeks

BBL results deepen over four to eight weeks as your skin rebuilds collagen. We'll check in at the six-week mark — by phone or in person — to assess results and talk through next steps. No charge for the follow-up.
