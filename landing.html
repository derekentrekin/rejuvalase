Common Questions

Everything you need
to know before booking.

BBL Photofacial is most effective for Fitzpatrick skin types I–III (fair to medium skin tones) without active tan. If you have a darker complexion or recent sun exposure, we'll review your candidacy during booking and can substitute an equally valued treatment at the same event price. HydraFacial is appropriate for all skin types, including sensitive skin.

Avoid sun exposure for 2 weeks prior. Discontinue retinoids 5–7 days before your visit. Arrive with a clean face — no makeup or heavy products. Stay well hydrated. We'll send a complete pre-treatment guide when you book. If you have any active skin conditions, medications, or concerns, let us know and we'll advise accordingly.

HydraFacial has zero downtime. BBL may cause mild redness and warmth for a few hours post-treatment, similar to a light sunburn. Some clients experience darkening of pigmented spots over the following 7–10 days as the treated pigment rises and flakes naturally — this is the desired response, not a complication. Most clients return to normal activities the same day, with cosmetics worn starting the following morning.
