Our story

Founded 2019

Rejuvalase was founded in 2019 by Dr. Maya Chen, MD, with a straightforward purpose: bring hospital-grade aesthetic medicine to Northern Virginia without the opacity of a large chain or the pressure of a sales-driven environment. Stafford and the broader Prince William corridor had grown into a vibrant, discerning community — and it deserved a practice where patients could ask hard questions and receive honest answers.

The name blends rejuvenate and laser, but our mission reaches further than any single technology. We believe aesthetic medicine works best when it is individualized, evidence-based, and delivered by clinicians who remain current — not by technicians following a protocol sheet. Every treatment plan at Rejuvalase is developed with physician oversight. We do not rush consultations, and we do not pad treatment plans.

Five years on, our team has grown to six clinicians and coordinators, our patient satisfaction holds at 4.9 across 2,400+ reviews, and many of our patients have been with us since our first week. We measure success not in revenue per visit but in the number of patients who return year after year — because they feel genuinely seen, not sold to. We remain proudly independent, with no private-equity ownership and no corporate quotas.

Standards & credentials

Medical-grade, by design.

Every provider at Rejuvalase holds active clinical licensure and participates in mandatory continuing medical education. Our protocols are written by our Medical Director and reviewed annually against the latest peer-reviewed literature. We use only FDA-cleared devices and pharmaceutical-grade injectables sourced directly from manufacturers — and we do not delegate injectable or laser procedures to unlicensed personnel, ever.
