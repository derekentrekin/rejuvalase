The Rejuvalase approach

A clearer picture of the beauty you already have.

We opened in 2019 because the women in our lives kept driving to DC, Richmond, or Northern Virginia to find the medspa care they trusted. They shouldn't have to.

— Dr. Maya Chen, MDFounder & Medical Director

Rejuvalase is an independent, physician-led medspa and dayspa serving Stafford and the surrounding counties. Every injectable is performed by a licensed RN or MD. Every laser is a medical-grade device. Every consultation is unhurried, honest, and free.

We're the practice that will tell you an expensive treatment isn't right for your skin. We publish our injector training hours annually, and we'll always send you home with less filler than you thought you needed — because the best compliment is you look well-rested, not you look done.
