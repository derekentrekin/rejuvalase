Preparation & Aftercare

Before, during,
and after your visit.

01 — Before

How to prepare for your appointment

A little preparation ensures you get the most from both treatments and that your skin responds optimally to BBL light energy.

  • Avoid sun exposure and tanning for 2 weeks prior
  • Discontinue retinoids 5–7 days before your appointment
  • Arrive with a clean face — no makeup or heavy moisturizer
  • Stay well-hydrated the day before and day of
  • Let us know about any current medications or skin conditions

02 — During

What your treatment session feels like

Both treatments are well-tolerated. Most clients read, listen to music, or simply relax during the session.

  • HydraFacial: gentle suction sensation, no discomfort
  • BBL: brief warmth with each light pulse, similar to a snap of a rubber band
  • Protective goggles worn throughout BBL treatment
  • Our team checks in with you throughout — comfort is the priority
  • Redness or warmth is normal and resolves quickly

03 — After

Caring for your skin post-treatment

Your skin has done something significant. A few simple steps in the days following protect your investment and enhance results.

  • Apply broad-spectrum SPF 30+ every morning for 2 weeks
  • Avoid direct sun, heat, and exercise for 24–48 hours
  • Use only gentle, fragrance-free cleansers for 3 days
  • Expect pigmented spots to darken then flake away over 7–10 days
  • Optimal results develop over 4–6 weeks as collagen rebuilds
