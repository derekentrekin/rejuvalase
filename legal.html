Accessibility

Rejuvalase is committed to ensuring that our website and physical location are accessible to everyone, including people with disabilities. We believe accessible healthcare and wellness services are not optional — they are a matter of dignity and inclusion.

Our commitment

We are actively working to make rejuvalase.com conform to the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 Level AA. These guidelines describe how to make web content more accessible to people with disabilities, including those with visual, auditory, cognitive, and motor impairments.

Our ongoing accessibility efforts include:

  • Providing text alternatives for non-text content (images, icons, graphics)
  • Ensuring sufficient color contrast throughout the site
  • Supporting keyboard navigation and screen reader compatibility
  • Writing clear, descriptive link text and form labels
  • Avoiding content that flashes at rates known to trigger photosensitive conditions
  • Providing captions for video content where applicable

Physical accessibility

Our Stafford office is located on the second floor of 123 Garrisonville Rd and is accessible by elevator. Accessible parking spaces are available in front of the building. If you have any access needs — mobility, hearing, vision, or otherwise — please let us know when booking and we will make every accommodation possible.
